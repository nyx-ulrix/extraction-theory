Privacy policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

What the app stores

Your brews, beans, gear and settings are stored in one Google Sheet named "Extraction Theory" in your own Google Drive. The app creates this sheet the first time you sign in. You own it and can open, edit, share or delete it like any other file.

Google access

The only Google scope the app requests is drive.file. With it, the app can see only files it created or files you opened with it. It cannot read the rest of your Drive, your email or your contacts.

Extraction Theory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

No server, no tracking

  • There is no Extraction Theory server. The app runs in your browser and talks to Google directly.
  • No analytics, no cookies and no ads.
  • Nobody but you, and anyone you share the sheet with, can see your data.

This browser's local storage

Your browser keeps a few things on this device only: your theme choice, an unsaved brew draft, the ID of your sheet, and optional AI API keys.

AI keys are never written to the sheet. A key is sent only to the provider you choose (Claude, OpenAI or Gemini), and only to pick a colour for each tasting note. The note names (not your other data) are sent to that provider at the same time. That provider's own privacy policy applies to that request.

Revoke access and delete your data

  • Remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete your data by deleting the "Extraction Theory" sheet from Google Drive.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser to remove local settings and keys, or clear a key in Settings.
